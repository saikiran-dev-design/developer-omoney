Use case 01

Ooredoo Money as a payment method

Pre-authorise a wallet debit, redirect the customer to Ooredoo's hosted 3D Secure page, and receive the outcome on your receipt URL. An optional tokenisation layer lets returning customers skip mPin/OTP entirely.

1

Customer selects Ooredoo Money

Customer enters their mobile number on your checkout page.

2

Call debitWallet

You send merchant credentials, mobile number, and amount. Ooredoo checks wallet status, balance, and limits.

3

Redirect to 3D Secure

If 3DS is configured, the response includes a targetURL — redirect the customer there with the returned transactionId as paymentId.

4

Customer authenticates

Customer confirms with mPin and OTP on Ooredoo's hosted page — or, for returning customers with a saved token, this step is skipped entirely.

5

Receive the outcome on your return URL

Ooredoo redirects to the return URL configured for your merchant profile, with responseCode, responseDesc, and a verifiable hash. Optionally poll getTxnStatus.

Redirect / return URL: this is not sent on every API call — it's configured once against your merchant profile by the Ooredoo integration team during onboarding. Share your HTTPS return URL (separately for UAT and Production) as early as possible in the technical integration phase — without it, the 3DS flow can't be certified during UAT.

Partner onboarding

Getting a merchant live, step by step

Five phases take a merchant from first conversation to go-live. Here's what happens at each stage, and what's expected from your side.

1

Pre-onboarding preparation

  • You provide business profile and KYC documentation (trade licence, bank details, compliance forms).
  • Ooredoo Money validates your eligibility against product and compliance policy.
  • You confirm points of contact for product, finance, operations, and technology.
2

Commercial agreement

  • Merchant discount rate (MDR), processing fees, and any onboarding charges are finalised.
  • Settlement process and reporting are agreed and validated.
  • Commercial terms are signed by both parties.
3

Merchant enablement & training

  • Ooredoo Money walks you through the features of OM as a payment method.
  • Training on the Customer Support Tool (CST) — transaction monitoring, refunds, reversals.
  • You get access to reporting: transaction logs, settlement reports, reconciliation dashboards.
4

Technical integration

  • Ooredoo Money shares API specifications and a Postman collection with worked examples.
  • You provide the public IP(s) to whitelist for UAT and Production.
  • You provide the redirect / return URL(s) for the 3D Secure flow, for both UAT and Production.
  • Joint UAT is performed and validated: payment authorisation flow, refund/reversal flow, and settlement reconciliation.
  • Ooredoo Money approves the move to Production once UAT is signed off.
5

Go-live & post-onboarding

  • Go-live is confirmed and the first week of transactions is monitored closely.
  • Any integration, reconciliation, or settlement issues are resolved proactively.
  • A periodic (monthly or quarterly) review is scheduled between Ooredoo Money and you.
